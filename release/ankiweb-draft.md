Chat With Your Cards adds a review-aware assistant beside Anki. Ask about the
current card, search for prerequisites and related notes, inspect your
collection, and review proposed changes before anything is applied.

The dock stays inside Anki and supports ordinary chat, card explanations,
collection tools, reversible proposals, and explicit approval for changes.
Destructive operations use Anki-native undo and backup safeguards.
